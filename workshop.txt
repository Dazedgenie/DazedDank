description=[list]
description=[*]Cannabis is a normal farm crop: plow, use [b]Sow Seed[/b], water, harvest. It grows indoors too.
description=[*]Five stages. Feed [b]Veg[/b] food in veg and [b]Bloom[/b] food in flower, once per stage; twice burns the plant.
description=[*]Care, light, genetics and harvest timing set the final quality.
description=[*][b]Inspect Cannabis Plant[/b] opens a status window with stage, water, health and warnings.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-light.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/lights.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Ceiling lamps hang indoors; flood lights stand anywhere. Both need power.
description=[*]Add a [b]Light Timer[/b]: [b]18/6[/b] holds plants in veg, where every extra day adds yield. Switch to [b]12/12[/b] to flower.
description=[*]Veg light spilling onto a 12/12 plant in its dark hours is a [b]light leak[/b]: stress, and maybe a hermie.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-bags.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Small and Large Grow Bags[/b] place like furniture and become plots. Fill them with potting soil once and they're reusable.
description=[*]Fabric forgives overwatering. A large bag gives 25% more buds.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-hydro.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/hydro.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Three systems: [b]DWC[/b] buckets, [b]RDWC[/b] with up to 6 sites on one reservoir, and [b]Ebb and Flow[/b] flood tables on a timer.
description=[*]Indoor only. Plants drink and eat from a reservoir: top it up, dose it, change it.
description=[*]A dead air pump, stale or tainted water brings [b]root rot[/b]. Catch it early: change the reservoir and treat with bleach.
description=[*]With [b]Dazed Plumbing[/b], pipe a reservoir to a water tank and changes refill themselves.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-breed.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/strains.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Six starter strains, each with hidden potency, yield and flowering speed. Cross two and you get a brand-new named strain.
description=[*]Indica, Sativa and Hybrid, each with their own look, high and genetics. About 1 seed in 10 is male.
description=[*]Top plants in veg for more buds, and keep an [b]XL mother pot[/b] for steady cuttings.
description=[*]Males show at pre-flower. [b]Pull Male Plant[/b], or keep one near your females to make seeds of the cross.
description=[*]Stressed females can turn [b]hermie[/b] and seed themselves and every female nearby.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-clone.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Take Cutting[/b] in veg or pre-flower with scissors or a knife. Dip it in [b]rooting gel[/b].
description=[*]Root cuttings in a [b]Cloning Dome[/b], or stick them straight in soil. Skill, gel, warmth and light raise the odds.
description=[*]Clones keep their mother's genetics, with a little drift each generation.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-cure.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/curing.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Hang wet plants on a [b]Drying Rack[/b]; a [b]Drying Fan[/b] helps. Leave them too long and they over-dry.
description=[*]Cure in a [b]Curing Jar[/b] (30 buds) or [b]Curing Barrel[/b] (300). Burp every day and a half or risk mold.
description=[*]Buds are named by quality: Poor, Average, Good, Premium, and [b]Top Shelf[/b] from RDWC.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/DazedDank-Workshop/main/images/h-smoke.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Roll Joint[/b] with papers, or [b]Smoke in Pipe[/b] for a harder hit. You need a lighter or an open flame.
description=[*]Indica relaxes and makes you hungry; Sativa lifts your mood and wakes you up. Moldy smoke only hurts.
description=[*]Tolerance builds with every dose. Regular use builds dependency, and going without brings withdrawal.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Grow rooms[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Seal a room and hang a [b]Grow Room Panel[/b]: one light schedule and one dashboard for every lamp and reservoir inside.
description=[*]Each room has its own [b]temperature and humidity[/b]. Fans, heaters, dehumidifiers and humidifiers keep it in range.
description=[*]Open doors and bare windows leak light. Hang a sheet to seal them.
description=[/list]

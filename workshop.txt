Every step matters to the quality of what you harvest. Made by a working grower for [b]Build 42[/b].

description=[list]
description=[*]Cannabis is a normal farm crop: plow, [b]Sow Seed[/b], water, harvest. It grows indoors too.
description=[*]Feed [b]Veg[/b] food in veg and [b]Bloom[/b] food in flower, once per stage. [b]Top Plant[/b] for more buds.
description=[*][b]Strip Fan Leaves[/b] in late veg and early flower for better buds. Strip too often and the plant suffers.
description=[*][b]Inspect Cannabis Plant[/b] opens a status window with stage, water, health and warnings.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-light.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/lights.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Ceiling lamps hang indoors; flood lights stand anywhere. All need power.
description=[*]A [b]Light Timer[/b] runs [b]18/6[/b] to hold plants in veg, where extra days add yield, or [b]12/12[/b] to flower.
description=[*]Veg light reaching a 12/12 plant in its dark hours is a [b]light leak[/b]: stress, and maybe a hermie.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-bags.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/bags.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Small, Large and XL Grow Bags[/b] place like furniture. Fill them with potting soil once and they're reusable.
description=[*]A [b]drip tank[/b] keeps soil pots evenly watered.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-hydro.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/hydro.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]DWC[/b] buckets, [b]RDWC[/b] with up to 6 sites on one reservoir, and [b]Ebb and Flow[/b] flood tables on a timer.
description=[*]Plants drink and eat from the reservoir: top it up, dose it, change it.
description=[*]A dead air pump or stale water brings [b]root rot[/b]. Catch it early: change the water and treat with bleach.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-breed.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/strains.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Six starter strains with hidden potency, yield and flowering speed. Cross two and you get a new named strain.
description=[*]Males show at pre-flower. [b]Pull Male Plant[/b], or keep one near your females for seeds.
description=[*]Stressed females can turn [b]hermie[/b] and seed the whole room. Cold nights in late flower can turn buds [b]purple[/b].
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-clone.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/cloning.png?v=2[/img]
description=Clones keep their mother's genetics, with a little drift each generation. Keep a mother in an XL pot for steady cuttings.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-rooms.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/rooms.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/roomgear.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Hang a [b]Grow Room Panel[/b] in a closed room: one light schedule and one dashboard for every lamp, plant and reservoir inside.
description=[*]With the Garden Crops add-on, vanilla crops growing in the room's containers show up under View all in an Other crops list.
description=[*]Set the room to Veg, Flower or Drying. Fans, AC, heater and (de)humidifiers hold it in range on their own.
description=[*]Open doors and bare windows leak light. Hang a sheet to seal them.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-cure.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/curing.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Hang wet plants on a [b]Drying Rack[/b]. Leave them too long and they over-dry; damp and rain bring mold.
description=[*]Cure in a [b]Curing Jar[/b] or [b]Curing Barrel[/b]. Burp every day and a half or risk mold.
description=[*]Vanilla dried tobacco cures in a jar or barrel too, and shreds into double the loose tobacco.
description=[*]Buds are named by quality, up to [b]Top Shelf[/b] from RDWC. Fan leaves dry and cure into hemp wraps.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/h-smoke.png?v=2[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dazedgenie/Dazed-Workshop/main/Dank/images/smoking.png?v=2[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Roll Joint[/b], [b]Smoke in Pipe[/b], or [b]Roll Blunt[/b] in a hemp wrap or tobacco leaf. A blunt lasts 3 sessions: put it out and relight it later.
description=[*]Tolerance builds with every dose. Regular use builds dependency, and going without brings withdrawal.
description=[/list]

With Dazed Power 0.8.5+ (and Dazed Core 1.8.4), grow lights, room fans, heaters, ACs, (de)humidifiers, hydro and drip pumps and the drying fan can be cabled to a controller or transformer as Power loads: billed on the LOADS page, shown in Load Priority, dark or stopped when cut, no controller slot. Gear you do not cable keeps running off any powered square, free.

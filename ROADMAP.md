# Dazed Dank -- roadmap

## Ideas (added 2026-10-10, from the community scan; not designed or coded)

- Hemp fibre crop: grow, ret and process hemp into rope, twine and burlap (Climate's frost table already lists hemp).
- Tobacco farming: grow tobacco and reuse Dank's drying and curing chain for the leaf, rolled into cigarettes for
  vanilla smokers.
- Vanilla crops in grow bags and hydro: design agreed 2026-10-04, see [docs/vanilla-crops.md](docs/vanilla-crops.md).

  list is still to do.

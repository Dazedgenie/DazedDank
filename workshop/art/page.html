4HARVEST & DRY

RackCheck Drying Rack

Hang the wet plant on a drying rack. A fan nearby speeds it up and keeps mold away.

5TRIM & CURE

JarBurp Jar

Trim the dry plant into buds, then cure them in a jar or barrel. Remember to burp it.

6SMOKE

BudRoll Joint

Roll a joint or pack a pipe. Better buds hit harder and last longer.

INDICA

Short and bushy. A heavy body high: stress and unhappiness melt, you get sleepy and hungry.

SATIVA

Tall, with a long cola. An up high that kills boredom and wakes you up. Too strong makes you anxious.

HYBRID

A bit of both. Cross an Indica with a Sativa to breed hybrid seeds.

MALE

No buds. Shows its sex at pre-flower: pull it, or keep it to pollinate females for seeds.
